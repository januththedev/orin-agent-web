Self-hosting

This website connects to your local gateway — the agent executes on your own machine or server. Your tasks never leave your infrastructure unless you choose otherwise.

Why local

  • Your models — point the gateway at the providers you already pay for.
  • Your files — code, documents and credentials stay on your disk.
  • Your network — no task content transits third-party agent clouds.

Open source and free. The workspace app on this site is the control surface; the gateway is the engine.
